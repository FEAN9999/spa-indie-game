# Phây lô A (bảng tin) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** An in-game social feed, Phây: real shop events become saved posts with likes, comments and a follower count with milestones, read in a scrolling phone panel opened from a button under the top bar, with slide-down notices.

**Architecture:** One `FeedBook` lives on the main `SpaSim` and is shared by reference with branches (like the purse). Posts are made inside the sim at the moment things happen, so nothing depends on which events reach the scene; text is composed once from templates and stored. The scene only polls the book: unread count, new posts to notify, milestones to cheer. Lô B (replies, boosts) needs no save change, so every field it uses is saved now.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Phaser 3, Vitest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-phay-design.md` (sections 4.1–4.8, 4.11, 5, 7 lô A, 8).

## Global Constraints

- Save version 36 (`SAVE_VERSION = 36`); `tests/fixtures/save-v35.json` is the frozen previous fixture (already in the repo).
- Every Phây draw uses its own stream `createRng(this.day * 9431 + this.nextId + salt)`; never `this.rng`. `tests/sim/same-draws.test.ts` must pass unchanged.
- Phây opens with `featureOpen('quests')` (day 8, earlier through the starter chain); before that no button and no posts.
- 50 posts kept; at most 3 praise and 3 complaint posts a day; followers never go down; milestones 1.000 / 10.000 / 100.000 / 1.000.000.
- Text: Vietnamese, no emoji, each post text at most 120 characters, stored whole in the post.
- A real star never complains in their own name: their complaints are posted by "Quản lý của [tên]". VIPs complain under their own name.
- Branch customers never post; branch milestones (opened, house bought) post into the shared feed.
- Phây never changes money, customers, stars or arrivals in lô A.

## Review Focus

1. Reload mid-day: no notice for posts made before the reload; the red count is the same before and after (`lastSeen` saved). → Task 6 test `no notice for posts already there on load`.
2. Owner at a branch while the main shop posts (idol, complaint): posts still made, notices still shown at the branch. → Task 4 test `posts while the owner is at a branch`.
3. A VIP/idol walk-out writes three 1s; after more than 20 reviews `reviews` and `reviewIds` stay aligned in memory and after save/load. → Task 3 test `reviewIds stay aligned past the window`.
4. Old save before day 8: followers 0, opens to 50 at day 8; old save past day 8: seeded followers, milestones below it marked done with no cheer cards. → Task 3 tests.
5. A drag that starts on a post's area never counts as a tap; long regular/idol names fit the post header. → Task 5 tests on `isDrag` and `fitName`.
