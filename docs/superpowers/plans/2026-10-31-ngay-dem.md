# Ngày và đêm Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The shop's light follows the game clock, and at night its lamps glow.

**Architecture:** A pure `daylight(minutes)` gives the overlay colour, its alpha and how lit the lamps are. `RoomView.setDaylight` draws one overlay over the map (like the power cut's dark), a warm additive wash over the shop's rows, and an additive `fx-glow` at each lamp on the floor in view. `SpaScene.update` calls it each frame.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Phaser 4, Vitest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-ngay-dem-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No save change; the light is derived from `sim.clock.minutes`.
- The day stays 8:00–20:00.
- Only the shop map darkens: not mini-games, cards, bubbles, floating money or the HUD.

## Review Focus

- After closing (20:00, day over) the shop stays at full night until the next day starts at 8:00.
- During a power cut, lamps stay off.
- Lamps on another floor, a carried item (build mode) and a turned bench's mat never glow.
- A flipped item's lamp glows on the mirrored side.
- Bought neighbour houses are covered by the overlay too.
