# Đợt 2 Lô B: VIP Guests Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Four named VIPs book a visit for tomorrow once the shop has two technicians. Each one arrives on time with her own look and a fanfare. Only the owner serves her, the hard mini-game is played, and she pays ×3 and reviews ×3. Her first 3★ visit gives a signed photo, and all four photos give the gold board. The guest book gains a VIP page. Save version 9 keeps all of it, and every older shop opens with no booking.

**Architecture:**
- **`src/sim/vips.ts`** is a new pure module. It holds the four VIPs' fixed data, the `VipBook` state and the rules: booking, who is due, scoring.
- **`SpaSim` owns a `vip: VipBook`:**
  - It books at the end of a day.
  - It spawns the booked VIP at her minute as an extra arrival.
  - She stands by the desk when every seat is taken.
  - It scores her in `payFor`.
- **`crew.ts`:** staff never fetch a VIP, and they hold her service's beds while she waits.
- **The view:**
  - each VIP's own accessories;
  - a gold star over her;
  - the booking on the summary card, a morning toast and a star by the clock;
  - a VIP tab in the guest book.
- **Photos and the gold board** are gift furniture and go through the lot A gift queue (`book.gifts`).

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Phaser ^4.2.1 (view only), Vite ^8.3, Vitest. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-dot-2-khach-design.md` §7, Lô B, approved 2026-10-27. Also §2, §4 and §8. Lô A (§6) is live and is the base.

**Branch:** `dot-2-vip`, cut from master `4cecdfc`, where lô A is live.

## Global Constraints

- **Layering:**
  - `src/sim/**` never imports Phaser, `src/view/**`, `src/scenes/**`, `src/art/**` or `src/audio/**`.
  - `src/strings/vips.ts` imports only types.
- **All player-facing text lives in `src/strings/`.**
  - UI strings go in `vi.ts`.
  - The VIPs' names, roles and lines go in a new `src/strings/vips.ts`.
  - Every speech line is at most **60 characters** (`BUBBLE_MAX_CHARS`).
- **Names:** Mây Chiều, Ngân Sa, Bích Câu and Tám Lửa are invented. A web search on 2026-10-27 found no real celebrity with any of these names (spec §4). Do not change them.
- **Save:**
  - `SAVE_VERSION = 9`.
  - Saves of versions 1–8 still open as the same shop, with `emptyVipBook()` and no booking.
  - A damaged `vip` is repaired field by field. It is never a reason to lose the shop.
  - An unknown VIP id on a customer becomes `null`.
- **RNG:**
  - VIP booking draws only from its own stream, `createRng(day * 7919 + nextId)`, made at the moment of booking. It never draws from the sim's main `rng`.
  - Spawning a VIP draws nothing, unless her service is gone (then `drawService`, like a regular).
  - Every existing seeded test must pass unchanged.
- **Only the owner serves a VIP.** Staff never fetch one. While a VIP is in the shop and not yet sent to a bed, staff take no customer of her service.
- **A VIP never walks out for lack of a seat.** If every seat is taken she stands by the reception desk.
- **Booking starts** at the end of a day when the shop has at least 2 technicians who are not leaving and at least one VIP's service is available. Once made, a booking holds even if that stops being true.
- **Style A:** cream cards with brown borders (`addCard`), buttons at least 120 px tall (`addButton`), and all text through `t()` or `src/strings/vips.ts`.
- **Numbers (config.ts):** copy them exactly.

  ```ts
  /** VIPs begin booking once the shop has this many technicians who are not leaving. */
  export const VIP_MIN_TECHNICIANS = 2
  /** A booked hour falls on a half hour between these game minutes: 10:00 and 17:00. */
  export const VIP_FROM_MIN = 600
  export const VIP_TO_MIN = 1020
  /** Days from one VIP visit to the day of the next, at least and at most. */
  export const VIP_GAP_DAYS = [2, 4] as const
  export const VIP_PRICE_FACTOR = 3
  export const VIP_REVIEWS = 3
  /** A VIP's patience against PATIENCE_MS; the early days' extra patience does not apply. */
  export const VIP_PATIENCE = 0.7
  /** The clock's gold star shows from this many game minutes before the booked time. */
  export const VIP_WARN_MIN = 60
  ```

## Review Focus

1. **A VIP arrives to a full shop, or her spot gets covered by furniture.** She stands by the desk, never walks out for lack of a seat, and the owner can tap her there. If a build change covers her spot, she walks to a new one. Tests: Task 4, `stands a VIP by the desk when every seat is taken` and `finds a standing VIP a new spot when furniture covers hers`.
2. **The game is reloaded while a VIP is booked, standing or on the bed.** She stays the same VIP, standing stays standing, and a booking still brings her exactly once. Tests: Task 3, `keeps the VIP book and a VIP customer through a round trip`; Task 4, `brings a booked VIP once after a reload` and `keeps a standing VIP standing after a reload`.
3. **Staff and a waiting VIP.** A technician never fetches her and never takes a bed of her service while she waits, even when other customers have waited longer. Once she is on her way to a bed, staff go back to work. Test: Task 4, `staff never fetch a VIP and leave her service's beds to her`.
4. **The shop changes after the booking.** If the booked service's bed is sold, she asks for a service the shop still has. If the technicians are let go, she still comes, but no new booking is made. Tests: Task 4, `asks for a service the shop has when hers is gone` and `still comes after the technicians leave, but books no one new`.
5. **An old shop on the new version.** It opens with no booking. The first booking is made at the end of its first eligible day, and the VIP tab shows only from then. Tests: Task 3, `opens every older save with an empty VIP book`; Task 4, `books an old shop's first VIP at the end of its first eligible day`; Task 7, `shows the VIP tab only once a VIP has been booked`.

  ```ts
  export type VipId = 'singer' | 'miss' | 'star' | 'chef'
  /** Book order and the order they are first booked in. */
  export const VIP_IDS: VipId[] = ['singer', 'miss', 'star', 'chef']
  export interface VipSpec { look: Look; service: ServiceId; photo: ItemKind }
  export const VIPS: Record<VipId, VipSpec> = {
    singer: { look: { skin: 0, hair: 0, hairStyle: 3, shirt: 3 }, service: 'massage', photo: 'photoSinger' },
    miss: { look: { skin: 1, hair: 0, hairStyle: 1, shirt: 0 }, service: 'mask', photo: 'photoMiss' },
    star: { look: { skin: 1, hair: 2, hairStyle: 3, shirt: 1 }, service: 'mask', photo: 'photoStar' },
    chef: { look: { skin: 2, hair: 0, hairStyle: 2, shirt: 4 }, service: 'shampoo', photo: 'photoChef' },
  }
  /** Underneath, every VIP is a celebrity: her tips, her harsh grading and her pace. */
  export const VIP_KIND: CustomerKind = 'celeb'
  export interface VipState { visits: number; best: number; photo: boolean }
  /** Who comes, on which day and at which game minute; the service is the VIP's own. */
  export interface Booking { vip: VipId; day: number; minute: number }
  export interface VipBook { vips: Record<VipId, VipState>; booking: Booking | null; nextDay: number; everBooked: boolean; boardGiven: boolean }
  export function emptyVipBook(): VipBook          // visits 0, best 0, photo false; booking null; nextDay 0; everBooked false; boardGiven false
  export function vipEligible(technicians: number, available: ServiceId[]): boolean
  export function planBooking(book: VipBook, day: number, technicians: number, available: ServiceId[], rng: Rng): Booking | null
  export function dueVip(book: VipBook, day: number, minutes: number): Booking | null
  export function clearStale(book: VipBook, day: number): void
  export function scoreVip(book: VipBook, gifts: ItemKind[], id: VipId, stars: Stars): { photo: boolean; board: boolean }
  ```

- **Rules:**
  - `vipEligible`: `technicians >= VIP_MIN_TECHNICIANS` and some VIP's service is in `available`.
  - `planBooking` books for `day + 1`. It returns `null` without touching `book` or drawing from `rng` when any of these holds: a booking is pending; `day + 1 < book.nextDay`; or the shop is not eligible. Otherwise it draws in this order:
    1. **Who.** The first VIP in `VIP_IDS` with `visits === 0` whose service is available. This draws nothing. If there is none, `rng` picks evenly among every VIP whose service is available.
    2. **Minute.** `VIP_FROM_MIN + 30 * Math.floor(rng() * ((VIP_TO_MIN - VIP_FROM_MIN) / 30 + 1))`.
    3. **Gap.** `VIP_GAP_DAYS[0] + Math.floor(rng() * (VIP_GAP_DAYS[1] - VIP_GAP_DAYS[0] + 1))`. Set `book.nextDay = day + 1 + gap`.

    Then it sets `book.booking`, sets `everBooked = true`, and returns the booking.
  - `dueVip`: returns the booking when `booking.day === day && booking.minute <= minutes`, else `null`.
  - `clearStale`: drops a booking whose day is before `day`.
  - `scoreVip`:
    - `best = max(best, stars)`.
    - On the first 3★, set `photo = true` and push `VIPS[id].photo` onto `gifts`.
    - When every VIP has her photo and `!boardGiven`, set `boardGiven = true` and push `'goldBoard'`.
    - Return what happened.

  - `waits 2 to 4 days from a visit to the next`: `nextDay` is in `[8, 10]` after booking day 6. With the booking cleared, `planBooking` on day 6 returns null.
  - `picks among every VIP with an open service once all have visited`: every `visits = 1` and `['shampoo']` always gives `'chef'`.
  - `is due only on its day from its minute`.
  - `drops a booking for a day gone by`.
  - `keeps the best stars and gives each photo once`: 3★ twice pushes the photo once; 2★ later keeps best 3.
  - `gives the gold board once, when all four photos are in`.
