# Đợt 3 phần 2, lô A: lễ theo tháng — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Six month-long festivals (from day 8) that bring more, more patient customers, a festival service at +20%, a festival quest that gives a one-time keepsake, bunting and a sign in the room, the festival in the top bar, a "today" line, and save v17.

**Architecture:** A festival is a pure function of the day (`festivalOf`). Its effects are factors multiplied into the formulas `SpaSim` already has (arrival gap, patience, service draw, price). The festival quest is the month quest in a festival month, rolled by `rollMonth`; `payQuests` gives the keepsake once and records it in a new saved `festival` block. The view reads all of it from the sim.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Phaser 4 (view only), Vite, Vitest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-dot-3-le-su-kien-design.md` (sections 5, 7.1–7.3, 7.9, 8.1–8.3, 8.6, 9 lô A, 10, 11).

## Global Constraints

- `src/sim/**` never imports Phaser. Every player-facing string goes in `src/strings/vi.ts`, every tuning number in `src/config.ts`.
- Numbers: `FESTIVAL_FROM_DAY = 8`, `FESTIVAL_ARRIVAL = 0.8`, `FESTIVAL_PATIENCE = 1.2`, `FESTIVAL_SERVICE_CHANCE = 0.5`, `FESTIVAL_PRICE = 1.2`, `FESTIVAL_QUEST_SHARE = 0.5`, `KEEPSAKE_BEAUTY = 5`.
- No festival effect takes money, stars or items away (spec 5).
- Reloading must never pay a quest twice, give a keepsake twice, or re-announce a festival (spec 5).
- The shop rng's draws on days 1–7 do not change (`same-draws` keeps its recording, cut to 7 days).
- Text on screen must not overflow or overlap at the 1080×2160 game size (iPhone).
- Never stage `CLAUDE.md`. No attribution lines in commits.
- Test commands: `npx vitest run` (whole suite) and `npx tsc --noEmit`.

## Review Focus

1. Reload in a festival month (`fromSave(toSave())`, including on the summary where `fromSave` re-emits `dayEnded`): no second `festivalQuestDone`, no second keepsake, no `festivalStarted`. → Task 5 test "reloading after the festival quest pays nothing again".
2. The month's festival service loses its last bed mid-month: new customers must not be drawn for a service with no bed. → Task 3 test "never draws the festival service when it has no bed".
3. A young shop in a festival whose service it lacks (Noel with only shampoo): festival service is shampoo, the quest says Gội đầu, the card says why. → Task 1 test (`festivalService`) and Task 8 test (`festivalStartCard` swap line).
4. A v16 save loaded mid-festival month keeps its plain month quest: it pays as a plain `questDone`, the festival service falls back to `festivalService`, and the today line has no count. → Task 6 test "keeps an old month quest through a festival month" and Task 7 test (`todayLine` plain).
5. Long texts on iPhone: "Dịp Trung thu · Xông lá 12/12", the month header with a festival and the year label, the gold card's wrapped line. → Task 9 browser check.
